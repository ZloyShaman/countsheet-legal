Privacy Policy

CountSheet — Inventory Counting & Barcode Scanner

Google Play Privacy Information

Effective date: October 31, 2026
Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CountSheet ("the App") handles information. CountSheet is an Android application for inventory counting, barcode scanning, project management, and inventory data import and export.

CountSheet is published by TechAdvizor, an independent developer ("we", "us", or "our"). The App is designed to manage inventory data primarily on your device, without requiring a CountSheet account or developer-operated cloud storage.

1. Information Collection and Use

CountSheet allows you to create and manage inventory information, including:

  • Inventory projects and project names
  • Item names and descriptions
  • Barcodes and product identifiers
  • Item quantities and counting records
  • Data imported from files selected by you
  • Application settings and preferences

Inventory information is stored locally in the App's database on your device. CountSheet does not currently require user registration or automatically upload your inventory database to a developer-operated server.

Local inventory storage

Your projects, items, barcodes, and quantities are stored in the App's local database.

Third-party services

Google services used for advertising, purchases, and other functions may process technical information under their own policies.

2. Camera and Barcode Scanning

CountSheet may request camera permission to scan barcodes using your device's camera.

  • Camera access is used for barcode scanning and camera preview.
  • Camera permission is optional.
  • If camera access is denied or revoked, manual item and barcode entry remains available.
  • CountSheet does not intentionally upload camera images to a developer-operated server.

Barcode recognition uses Google ML Kit and Android camera technologies. Applicable third-party SDK processing is governed by the providers' privacy practices.

3. Local Data Storage

CountSheet stores inventory projects, items, counting records, and related information locally on your Android device.

Application settings and preferences are also stored locally. Certain settings may be eligible for Android's backup mechanisms, depending on your device configuration. The current App backup rules do not include the inventory database.

CountSheet does not currently provide automatic cloud synchronization or a developer-operated inventory storage service.

4. Import and Export of Files

CountSheet supports importing inventory information from selected files and exporting inventory data in supported formats.

The App uses Android's system file selection and document access features. You choose which files to import and where to save exported documents.

Exported files may contain inventory project names, item details, barcodes, quantities, and other information you entered.

If you share exported files through email, cloud storage, messaging applications, or other services, those services may process the files according to their own policies.

5. Advertising

The free version of CountSheet may display advertisements through Google AdMob when advertising is enabled and permitted.

Google's advertising services may process technical information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers, where permitted
  • Device and application information
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Advertising interactions and performance information
  • Diagnostic and fraud-prevention information

CountSheet uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable advertising consent and privacy choices.

Ad availability and personalization may depend on your location, consent choices, device settings, and applicable laws.

When the advertising provider is disabled, CountSheet does not request advertisements through that provider.

CountSheet Pro is designed to provide an ad-free experience within the App.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from partner apps.

6. In-App Purchases

CountSheet offers an optional one-time purchase called CountSheet Pro Lifetime through Google Play Billing.

Google Play handles payment processing. CountSheet does not directly receive or store your full payment card details, payment credentials, or billing address.

The App communicates with Google Play to check purchase ownership, restore purchases, and determine access to Pro features.

Purchase-related information may be processed or retained by Google according to its own policies.

7. Third-Party Services

CountSheet uses software and services provided by Google and Android platform components, including:

  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchase processing and verification.
  • Google AdMob — advertising when enabled.
  • Google User Messaging Platform — advertising consent and privacy choices.
  • Google ML Kit — barcode recognition.
  • Android CameraX — camera access and barcode scanning preview.

These services may process device, network, diagnostic, advertising, or purchase-related information as described in their applicable privacy policies.

8. Data Sharing

CountSheet does not sell your inventory projects or item records.

The App does not automatically send its local inventory database to a CountSheet-operated cloud service.

Third-party providers may process information for advertising, purchase verification, consent management, and related technical functionality.

You may also choose to export and share inventory files with external applications, services, or other people.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Inventory data remains in local App storage until you delete the relevant information, clear application data, or uninstall CountSheet, subject to Android backup and restore behavior.

Exported files saved outside the App's private storage may remain available after the App is uninstalled.

Google and other third-party providers may retain information according to their own data retention policies.

10. Security

CountSheet uses Android's application storage and permission mechanisms to help protect locally stored information.

However, no software or storage system can guarantee absolute security. We recommend protecting your device and keeping backup copies of important exported inventory information.

11. Children's Privacy

CountSheet is intended for inventory management, including use by adults, businesses, warehouses, and professional users.

The App is not designed specifically for children, and we do not knowingly seek to collect children's personal information through a CountSheet-operated account or cloud service.

12. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your device, region, and applicable laws, you may:

  • Grant or deny camera permission
  • Use manual barcode and item entry
  • Delete inventory projects and items
  • Clear application data or uninstall CountSheet
  • Manage available advertising privacy choices
  • Manage Android and Google advertising settings
  • Choose whether to export or share inventory files

For questions about information handled by the Developer, or to exercise applicable privacy rights, please contact us.

13. International Data Processing

Third-party providers such as Google may process technical, advertising, consent, or purchase-related information in countries other than your own, subject to their applicable policies and legal safeguards.

CountSheet does not automatically transfer your local inventory database to a developer-operated cloud server.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the App, third-party services, or applicable laws.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
